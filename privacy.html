Privacy notice

How project inquiry details are used.

Inquiry information

When you contact Western Electrical Engineering, the information you voluntarily provide may be used to discuss your project, prepare a proposal and respond to your inquiry.

The proposal request form sends information to a Google-hosted inquiry processing endpoint also used for the owner's existing PNW Electrical Engineering lead-intake system. During the brand transition, these inquiries may be delivered to an existing business inbox managed by the same owner. The information is used to review and respond to your project inquiry. Do not include Social Security numbers, financial information, passwords, or other sensitive personal data.

Analytics

This website uses browser session storage to retain limited advertising source parameters (such as campaign tags and click reference codes) while you move between our pages. If you submit a project inquiry, those parameters may be included in the inquiry so we can understand how you reached us. The site also prepares browser-side events for form submission attempts and telephone or email link clicks, but no third-party analytics or advertising conversion pixel is currently enabled. This notice will be updated before additional measurement tools are activated.

Contact

Questions about website inquiries can be sent to casey@westernengineer.com.

Review this draft notice before launch and update it to reflect actual data practices and applicable legal requirements.
